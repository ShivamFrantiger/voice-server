Batch Processing (REST API): The current code sends audio to ElevenLabs using a standard HTTP POST request (_sendToElevenLabs). This means it waits for your entire sentence to finish before sending it. In audioUtils.js (mulawToWav16k), you are using basic linear interpolation to artificially stretch the 8kHz audio to 16kHz. This introduces heavy aliasing distortion. ElevenLabs receives this distorted, muddy audio, struggles to understand it, and outputs a robotic or garbled voice.

Speech Threshold (speechThreshold: 200): If you speak softly, or if the phone line is slightly quiet, the RMS energy might drop below 200. The VAD will abruptly cut your sentence in half or drop soft syllables, making the output sound choppy.
